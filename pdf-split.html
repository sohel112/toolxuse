How to split a PDF

Upload your PDF, enter page numbers like 1-3,5 to extract those pages into one file — or leave empty to get every page as its own PDF. All processing happens in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract specific pages?

Enter page numbers like 1-3,5 in the pages field to extract those pages.

Can I split every page separately?

Yes — leave the pages field empty and each page becomes its own PDF file.
