    How to merge PDF files

    Select two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, and click Merge. Your files are combined entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is it free to merge PDF files?

    Yes, completely free with no limits on the number of files.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. All processing happens in your browser — your files never leave your device.

    Is there a watermark?

    No watermark is added to your merged PDF.
