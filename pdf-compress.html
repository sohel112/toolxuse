How PDF compression works

Each page is re-rendered as an optimized image at your chosen quality, then rebuilt into a new PDF. Works best on scanned or image-heavy PDFs. Text remains readable at 70% quality in most cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I reduce PDF size?

Typically 50-80% smaller depending on the original, especially for scanned PDFs.

Will quality be lost?

At 70% quality, text remains clearly readable. Lower the slider for smaller files.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No — compression runs entirely in your browser.
