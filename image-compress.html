How to compress a photo

Upload your image, choose quality (lower = smaller file), pick output format, and download. WebP gives the smallest size with great quality. Everything happens in your browser — your photos are never uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

How small can my photo become?

WebP at 60-70% quality is often 70-80% smaller than the original JPG.

Which format is smallest?

WebP gives the best size-to-quality ratio, followed by JPG.

Are my photos private?

Yes — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
